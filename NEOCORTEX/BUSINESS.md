> Scope: strategy, marketing copy, sales outreach, product specs, pricing · Pairs with: [[NEOCORTEX/WRITING|WRITING]], [[NEOCORTEX/THINKING|THINKING]], [[NEOCORTEX/PLANNING|PLANNING]], `PREFRONTAL` (brand and product facts)

## Core Rules (non-negotiable)

### RULE#1 Project context first
Read the project's brand and product notes; ask only for decisive facts that are missing (audience, offer, goal).

### RULE#2 Their problem, not your product
Lead with the reader's situation and outcome. One idea per section, one call to action (verb + what they get).

### RULE#3 Never invent proof
No made-up stats, quotes, testimonials, prices or results. Use `[PLACEHOLDER]` and flag it.

### RULE#4 Numbers over adjectives
Figures, timeframes and named results instead of "streamline", "optimize" or "innovative".

## Defaults
- Establish audience, pain, objections, desired action and proof before drafting.
- Follow channel conventions: length, subject and preview line, platform format.
- Mirror customers' own words (reviews, calls, tickets).
- Brand voice from the project; none on file: neutral, concise, professional.
- Draft with what you have; at most one blocking question.
- Strategy: name the bet, the metric that proves it and a counter-metric ([[NEOCORTEX/THINKING|THINKING]]).
- Specs and PRDs: Problem · Goals · Non-goals · P0/P1/P2 with acceptance criteria · Metrics · Open questions with owners.

## Avoid
- "Hope this finds you well", "I wanted to reach out", "Just checking in", fake "Re:" subjects.
- leverage, synergy, best-in-class, cutting-edge, world-class; exclamation marks.
- Feature dumps, several CTAs, "Learn more", "Click here", "Submit".
- Asking for a 30-minute call on first contact; `{{FirstName}}`-only personalization.
- Naming competitors unprompted.

## Output
- Copy by section (headline, subhead, body, CTA); 2–3 alternates only for headline, subject line and CTA; rationale only if asked.
- Cold email: body 120 words max, subject 2–7 words; sequences of 3–5 emails, each with a new angle.
